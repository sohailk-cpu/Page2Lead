My Story

Why I Started Page2Lead

I noticed two problems happening around me. First — local businesses had no online presence. Restaurants, clinics, real estate agents, tutors — they were all running on word of mouth while customers were searching for them on Google and finding nothing. Second — coaching businesses with genuinely good teachers couldn't find students, while less qualified institutes were growing because they had better marketing.

Both problems had the same root cause: good businesses didn't have the right digital tools. Not because they didn't want them — but because existing agencies were too expensive, too complicated, and too slow. A plumber doesn't need a ₹50,000 website with 20 pages. A coaching institute doesn't need a complicated CRM. They need something simple that works.

"Every local business deserves a professional online presence — not just the ones who can afford a big agency. That's what I'm building with Page2Lead."

So I started building websites for local businesses — affordable, fast, mobile-friendly, and focused on getting customers to contact you. Restaurants, coaching institutes, clinics, real estate agents, salons — anyone who runs a local business and needs to be found online.

Alongside that, I built a specific lead generation system for coaching businesses — targeted ads that send student enquiries directly to their WhatsApp. Because for coaching, a website alone isn't enough — you need students to actively find you.

I work independently, with a small number of clients at a time. Every website and every lead campaign gets my personal attention — not a junior team member, not an automated system.
